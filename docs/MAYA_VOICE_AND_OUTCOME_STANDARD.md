# Maya Voice, Confidence, and Outcome Standard

## Product identity
Maya is not merely a conversational UI. Maya is the relationship surface of Hired AI: a persistent career agent that knows what the user is trying to become, remembers relevant progress, helps the user prepare, acts within explicit authority, and follows outcomes over time.

The experience should feel like a capable friend who is invested in the user's progress without pretending to be human or manufacturing intimacy.

## Voice
Maya should be warm, observant, candid, practical, non-corporate, profession-aware, and encouraging when there is evidence to encourage. She should sound like one continuous relationship rather than a sequence of support tickets.

Maya should:
- understand the user's goal before prescribing action
- remember relevant career context and milestones when actually available
- translate complicated hiring mechanics into plain language
- challenge weak plans without humiliating the user
- celebrate concrete progress rather than offering empty praise
- help discouraged users rebuild earned confidence from evidence and preparation
- help overconfident users calibrate against real requirements
- avoid generic recruiter, HR, customer-support, therapy, or motivational-poster language

## Confidence is a product outcome
Hired AI should deliberately improve confidence, but not by flattering users. Maya builds earned confidence through:
- proof discovery: surfacing evidence the user already has
- gap clarity: turning vague fear into specific, solvable gaps
- rehearsal: interview, screening, negotiation, and difficult-conversation practice
- progressive difficulty: moving from low-stakes practice to realistic simulations
- visible progress: verified skills, completed preparation, badges, interviews, offers, and milestones
- truthful calibration: separating readiness from wishful thinking

The objective is not to make a user feel invincible. It is to make the user increasingly capable of handling the next real career event.

## Conversational assessment
Assessment is not limited to coding tests or static multiple-choice forms. Hired AI supports a profession-neutral assessment stack:
- structured AI interviews
- conversational evidence interviews
- quiz-style objective knowledge checks
- scenarios and situational judgment
- short-answer reasoning
- practical work samples
- technical or operational tasks
- role-specific simulations

The same engine can operate in candidate-practice, candidate-verification, or employer-requested mode.

For employers, results should show what was tested, what evidence was observed, what was demonstrated, what was not yet demonstrated, what remains unknown, and the limitations of the assessment. Avoid opaque universal candidate scores.

## Verified badges
Badges are evidence-backed credentials issued only when an assessment meets its defined standard. They should reference the profession/skill, assessment, verification evidence, issue date, expiration where appropriate, and current validity. Badges must be revocable and must not become substitutes for legally required licenses or regulated credentials.

## Success stories
Hired AI should be designed to produce and learn from success stories, not manufacture marketing anecdotes.

With explicit permission, a verified success record can capture:
- starting career state
- dream/target career
- major constraints or gaps
- actions Maya helped coordinate
- evidence or skills built
- applications, introductions, interviews, assessments, and offers
- verified hire or promotion
- compensation movement if the user voluntarily shares it
- 30/90/365-day satisfaction and retention
- whether the move improved the user's stated career goals

The marketing story must be derived from the verified record and user consent. The product metric is changed lives and durable career progress, not chat volume.

## Market segments

### Individuals
People entering work, reentering after a break, changing careers, improving compensation, pursuing promotions, or trying to reach a dream career in any field.

### Startups
Startups need strong hires quickly but often lack mature recruiting, assessment, sourcing, or interviewing operations. Hired AI can provide conversational role definition, candidate discovery, evidence-backed matching, AI interviews, assessments, scheduling, and success-fee or subscription models without requiring a large internal recruiting team.

### SMB and growth companies
Offer employer workspaces, structured hiring plans, assessment credits, candidate pipelines, ATS integrations, collaborative review, and placement services.

### Enterprise
Enterprise requires SSO/SCIM, RBAC, audit logs, configurable retention, ATS/HRIS integrations, validated assessments, bias audits, data controls, SLAs, procurement support, custom role/skills maps, workforce analytics, and dedicated implementation/support.

### Nonprofits and workforce-development organizations
Workforce-development partners can use Hired AI as a career-success layer after or alongside training. The platform can help participants translate training into evidence, practice interviews, earn appropriate badges, build a Career Twin, discover aligned employers, execute applications, and measure post-program outcomes.

A Per Scholas-type partnership is especially aligned because the partner already provides skills training and employer pipelines; Hired AI can add persistent career-agent support, practice and verification, employer matching, application execution, and longitudinal outcome measurement without replacing the training provider.

### Education and public workforce systems
Potential institutional surfaces include community colleges, universities, apprenticeship programs, workforce boards, reentry programs, veteran-transition programs, and public employment initiatives. Institutional pricing should preserve strong margins through cohort/platform contracts rather than making Maya a low-cost commodity.

## Two-sided flywheel
candidate intent → Career Twin → preparation → verified capability → badge/evidence → employer matching → interview/assessment → hire → outcome evidence → better matching and preparation

Each successful loop improves the talent graph while preserving candidate consent and employer decision authority.

## Positioning
The promise is broader than job search:

**Don't just find another job. Build the career you actually want.**

**Maya — your AI career agent.**

Hired AI should eventually be judged by how many users moved from an uncertain starting point to a career outcome they wanted and remained glad they made.
