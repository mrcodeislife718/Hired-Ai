# Indeed Parity and Reliability Hardening

Hired-AI must not become Indeed with a chatbot. It must preserve the useful employment-platform surfaces users expect, then exceed them with stronger evidence, explainability, personalization, professional-presence intelligence, mutual fit and longitudinal outcome learning.

## Current competitive baseline

The contemporary employment-platform baseline includes conversational/natural-language job discovery, profiles/preferences, resumes, application tracking, company/pay research, employer messaging, interview preparation and negotiation guidance on the candidate side. Employer tooling includes job posting, sponsored reach, screener questions, candidate matching, resume search/sourcing, messaging, interview coordination, hiring dashboards and integrations.

Hired-AI treats those as baseline surfaces rather than differentiation.

## Hired-AI superiority target

### Candidate side

- Freshness-verified and deduplicated opportunity inventory
- Explainable selective ranking rather than application-volume optimization
- Career Twin with structured preferences, trajectory, fulfillment and provenance
- Free + paid Resume Studio with evidence-backed claims
- GitHub career audit and repository organization
- LinkedIn/professional-social positioning
- Relationship/network graph and warm-path planning
- Role-specific recruiter, behavioral, technical, system-design and role-simulation interview practice
- Company/employer-quality research with uncertainty
- Salary/total-compensation intelligence
- Offer comparison and negotiation without fabricated leverage
- Career-development plans tied to real market gaps
- Post-hire 30/90/365-day outcomes, satisfaction, growth and Regret Rate

### Employer side

- Role calibration that distinguishes blockers from wishlist requirements
- Evidence-backed candidate matching
- Candidate sourcing by demonstrated capability and intent, not keyword count alone
- Mutual-fit and retention-compatible signals
- Explainable screening/manual-review paths
- Hiring-team collaboration and decision rationale
- Employer brand/culture/compensation evidence with provenance
- Messaging and interview coordination
- Hiring funnel and quality-of-hire learning
- Internal mobility and reskilling
- Employer Regret Rate and longitudinal quality-of-hire feedback

## Reliability laws

1. Deterministic/evidence-backed systems own consequential truth. LLMs explain and coordinate.
2. Cross-source duplicate jobs are canonicalized.
3. Stale or low-confidence opportunities require re-verification before outreach/application.
4. Material recommendation unknowns remain visible.
5. External actions are never called successful until independently confirmed.
6. Candidate and employer data are tenant isolated and purpose limited.
7. Employers cannot buy improved organic fit ranking.
8. Resume, profile and employer claims remain attributable.
9. Inferred Career Twin facts cannot masquerade as user-confirmed truth.
10. Free users receive the same baseline truthfulness, respect and care.

## Full surface standard

The canonical surface inventory and completion gates live in `docs/FULL_CAREER_SURFACE_STANDARD.md` and executable surface contracts live in `src/career-surfaces.ts`.

Each surface must progress through domain contract -> deterministic engine -> persistence -> API -> Maya routing -> UI -> authorization/privacy -> tests -> observability -> production verification -> outcome measurement before it is called production complete.
