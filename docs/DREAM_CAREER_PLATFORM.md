# Hired AI — Dream Career Platform

## North star

Everyone has a career they would rather have. Hired AI helps people discover it, become qualified for it, reach the people who can provide the opportunity, win it, and continue building after they are hired.

Maya is the persistent conversational Career Acquisition Agent. The primary interface is conversation rather than a collection of disconnected recruiting tools.

## Universal career coverage

Hired AI is profession-neutral. Technology is one vertical, not the default. The architecture supports healthcare, skilled trades, education, finance, legal, science, manufacturing, logistics, retail, hospitality, sales, marketing, creative work, media, public service, construction, transportation, energy, agriculture, nonprofit work, entrepreneurship, and roles not represented by a fixed taxonomy.

Every profession supplies its own requirements, acceptable evidence, mandatory gates, work samples, assessments, credentials, interview stages, compensation structure, and hiring practices. Required licenses, registrations, clearances, credentials, or legal authorizations remain hard gates.

## Conversational lifecycle

Maya should support natural requests such as:

- "I hate my current job but I don't know what else I qualify for."
- "I want to become a nurse, electrician, animator, sales leader, lawyer, engineer, teacher, or any other target profession."
- "Find strong roles and apply to the ones that satisfy my rules."
- "Why am I not getting interviews?"
- "Practice this interview with me."
- "I got an offer. Is it good?"

The lifecycle is:

career discovery → Career Twin → dream-career map → requirement decomposition → skill/proof gaps → development → verified proof → continuous opportunity discovery → direct introduction/referral/application → interview → negotiation → employment → 30/90/365-day outcome → next career move.

## Autonomous application execution

Maya may submit applications for a candidate only inside explicit authorization boundaries. The candidate can define constraints such as compensation, location, work mode, occupation, seniority, employer exclusions, required confidence, and when Maya must ask first.

Consequential or ambiguous questions remain approval-gated. Examples include legal attestations, sponsorship, relocation, security clearance, compensation commitments, demographic disclosures, and facts the evidence layer cannot verify.

External actions follow prepare → authorize → execute → verify → audit. Generated is not submitted; clicked is not delivered; an HTTP success is not proof that the employer received a valid application. The external-delivery ledger remains authoritative.

## Verified candidate assessments

Hired AI supports assessments for candidates and employers across occupations. Assessment types include structured interviews, realistic work samples, scenarios, technical tasks, communication, writing, analysis, role knowledge, problem solving, and other profession-specific demonstrations.

Assessment reports explain what was tested, why it matters, what the candidate demonstrated, where performance was weak, confidence, integrity evidence, role relevance, and what remains unknown. Hired AI does not reduce a person to one opaque quality score.

Assessment evidence feeds matching and career development. Maya can use the same evidence to identify the smallest high-value gap between a candidate and their target career.

## AI interviewer

The AI interviewer supports two modes:

1. **Practice** — candidate-controlled rehearsal with feedback and evidence-backed coaching.
2. **Employer-requested structured interview** — standardized competency questions and follow-ups based on a declared assessment definition.

Structured interviews are generated from role competencies rather than improvised personality judgment. Observations require evidence and preserve evaluator provenance (`ai`, `human`, or `hybrid`).

## Verified badges

Badges represent verified evidence, not decorative engagement points. A badge can only be issued after a passing assessment. It carries the candidate, profession, assessment, score, evidence digest, issue date, optional expiry, and revocation state.

Badges can therefore be independently tied back to the assessment evidence that produced them and can expire or be revoked when the underlying qualification is no longer current.

## Two-sided platform

### Candidate side

Maya owns career discovery, planning, qualification, proof building, professional positioning, resumes, portfolios, continuous opportunity search, direct introductions, applications, follow-up, practice interviews, employer research, compensation intelligence, negotiation, post-hire outcome tracking, and career advancement.

### Employer side

Employers get conversational role intake, requirement decomposition, talent search, Career Twin matching where consent permits, verified candidate evidence, assessments, AI interviews, structured scorecards, direct introductions, hiring collaboration, and outcome analytics.

The employer question Hired AI should answer is: **Who should I hire, and what evidence supports that recommendation?**

## Market differentiation

The product is not a resume editor plus auto-apply, not a single employer network, not an ATS with a chat box, and not a coding-test service.

Hired AI combines the career journey that competitors fragment across separate products:

- career discovery and transition
- persistent Career Twin
- continuous opportunity discovery
- direct introductions, referral paths, and verified applications
- multi-industry assessments
- AI interview practice and employer-side structured interviews
- verified evidence-backed badges
- compensation and negotiation
- career outcome learning

The moat is the longitudinal graph connecting ambitions → skills → verified capability → career history → employer requirements → applications → interviews → assessments → offers → compensation → hires → satisfaction → retention → advancement.

## Positioning and funnel

The transformation is larger than job search:

**Don't just find another job. Build the career you actually want.**

The funnel is:

Dream → Diagnosis → Plan → Proof → Opportunity → Acquisition → Career Growth.

The front door is conversation with Maya. The first result should provide immediate value: career diagnosis, possible paths, compensation position, target opportunities, and the highest-value gaps between the user and the career they want.

Hired AI is not positioned as the cheapest product. The reference catalog intentionally sits near the upper end of mainstream candidate tools while preserving much larger employer economics.

Candidate reference points:

Employer reference points:

- Employer Starter: $249/month plus 10% placement economics where Hired AI produces the placement
- Employer Pro: $499/month plus 10% placement economics
- Enterprise: $24,000/year minimum, with custom terms and 10% placement economics where applicable

These are reference prices, not hard-coded billing truth. Stripe and the environment-configured commercial catalog remain authoritative so production pricing can be changed without code releases.

The economic model supports subscriptions + placement + assessments + enterprise + usage + future marketplace revenue. Candidate subscriptions should not be the only or primary ceiling on company economics.

## Success metric

The platform should not optimize for application volume or even hiring alone. It should measure whether Hired AI produced a better career outcome: interview conversion, offer quality, compensation movement, time-to-hire, 30/90/365-day satisfaction, retention, advancement, and Regret Rate.
