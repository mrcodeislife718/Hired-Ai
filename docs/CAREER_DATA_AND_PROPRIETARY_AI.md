# Career Data, Insights, and Proprietary AI

Hired AI should compound from outcomes, not merely accumulate chat logs.

## Career insight network

The platform can collect consented, source-bound product events across career goals, applications, interviews, assessments, offers, hires, retention, advancement, and gig outcomes. Useful insight surfaces include conversion by role and channel, assessment-to-hire relationships, interview bottlenecks, compensation movement, career-transition paths, skill demand, employer response patterns, retention, advancement, gig-income patterns, and which interventions actually improve outcomes.

Analytics must remain privacy-conscious. Individual records are not market insight. Aggregate reporting should apply cohort thresholds, pseudonymous identifiers, consent, and exclusion of sensitive data. Protected-trait inference is not a product growth strategy.

## Proprietary Maya intelligence

The long-term goal is a proprietary Hired AI model family specialized in career intelligence, talent matching, assessment reasoning, employer decision support, career transitions, interviews, and Maya's conversational relationship style.

The training flywheel is:

company career knowledge + approved rubrics + verified outcome patterns + explicitly consented privacy-protected user-derived signals → curated dataset → holdout evaluation → governed model release → real-world outcome measurement → improved dataset.

Do not treat access to user conversations as blanket permission to train. Model-training use requires explicit permission. Sensitive material is excluded. Verified outcome-linked examples are more valuable than raw engagement because the company wants to learn what actually helped people achieve durable career progress.

Every proprietary model release should have dataset lineage, a content digest, versioning, holdout evaluations, bias and truthfulness checks, rollback, and documented authority boundaries. The model can become proprietary intelligence without becoming the authority for billing truth, verified credentials, application delivery, or other consequential deterministic state.

## Maya support modes

Maya can be a strong cheerleader when the user needs one. That does not mean permanent cheerleading. Maya adapts among steady support, stronger cheerleader mode, calibration, and celebration. The strongest encouragement should be tied to real strengths, progress, preparation, and winnable next steps. Support should increase user agency, not emotional dependence on Maya.

## Gig economy

Gig workers are first-class Hired AI users. Gig work can be an end goal, supplemental income, entrepreneurship path, or bridge into a salaried career. Hired AI should turn completed jobs, ratings, repeat customers, operational reliability, earnings, and work samples into portable evidence; help workers reduce platform concentration; identify higher-value gig paths; and translate gig experience into target-career language when users want to transition.

This does not collapse GigFlow into Hired AI. GigFlow remains a dedicated gig-economy product; Hired AI owns the worker's broader career relationship and can consume authorized gig evidence or opportunities through integrations.
