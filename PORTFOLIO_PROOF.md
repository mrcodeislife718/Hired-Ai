# Hired-AI — Portfolio Proof Contract

**Track:** AI Career Agent / commercial product

Hired-AI is complete only when its conversational career agent reliably improves measurable career outcomes across opportunity discovery, career intelligence, professional relationships, governed acquisition, career development, and outcome learning.

The product is not complete merely because it can search for jobs or prepare applications.

Required proof includes:

- end-to-end conversational career workflows
- strong qualified-opportunity precision
- evidence-grounded fit explanations
- truthful gap detection
- useful recruiter/hiring-manager/referral-path discovery
- relationship-memory and follow-up correctness
- governed application/outreach execution
- duplicate/spam prevention
- interview-preparation usefulness
- career-development recommendations tied to observed market gaps
- outcome-learning from no-response, rejection, screen, interview, offer, compensation, referral, and relationship evidence
- tests for agent boundaries, evidence, approvals, deduplication, retries, resumability, and degraded-provider behavior
- adversarial tests for hallucinated jobs/contacts, bad evidence, fabricated qualifications, duplicate applications, provider failure, unsafe outreach, and governance bypass
- benchmarks for time saved, workflow completion, conversion, cost, and recovery
- security, privacy, production deployment, and real-user outcome measurement

## Commercial proof targets

Hired-AI should measure:

- qualified-opportunity precision
- useful professional relationships created
- recruiter and hiring-manager conversations
- application-to-screen conversion
- screen-to-interview conversion
- interview-to-offer conversion
- time-to-interview
- time-to-offer
- compensation improvement
- user time saved
- retention after the immediate job search

## Next proof target

Run a fixed, audited user cohort through the conversational career workflow and compare opportunity quality, human intervention, networking outcomes, application conversion, interviews, time saved, errors, and offers against a manual/baseline workflow.

The long-term proof is behavioral and economic: users should increasingly prefer to ask their career agent rather than manually operate separate job boards, trackers, resume tools, networking tools, and career-planning products.
