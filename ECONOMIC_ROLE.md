# Hired-AI Economic Role

**Category:** Nearly sellable → direct-revenue business

## Economic objective
Convert Hired-AI / Maya from an engineering project into a customer-funded career operating system that is paid for because it improves a user's probability of reaching a real hiring decision.

## Target customer
Job seekers who are not getting adequate interview access from conventional application workflows, plus eventually workforce programs, career services organizations, and employers that value higher-quality candidate matching and engagement.

## Problem worth paying for
Conventional job search tools optimize discovery and application volume while candidates still fail to reach actual decision makers. Hired-AI should optimize job acquisition outcomes: opportunity discovery, evidence of fit, decision-maker access, readiness, relationship intelligence, outreach, interview preparation, and outcome learning.

## Current sellable capability
Maya as a conversational career agent; job/opportunity intelligence; readiness and career workflow concepts; governed acquisition workflow; resume/GitHub/social positioning; interview and relationship support.

## Distribution
Direct web product, founder-led acquisition, LinkedIn proof/content, workforce/career partnerships, referrals, and later B2B2C programs.

## Monetization
Direct subscription/usage tiers where justified, premium job-acquisition services, workforce-program contracts, employer/career-services partnerships, and enterprise licensing where appropriate. Monetization must reflect actual delivered value rather than arbitrary low-price subscriptions.

## Moat
Outcome-learning loop, decision-maker-oriented acquisition architecture, governed execution, persistent career context, evidence graph, and accumulated labor-market/career outcome intelligence.

## Immediate economic blockers
Reliable deployable product path, durable per-user data, authentication/entitlements, payment/contract path, privacy/export/delete controls, onboarding, clear offer packaging, and proof that Maya improves access to interviews or hiring decisions.

## Next highest-value action
Close only the launch blockers required to put Maya in front of real users and collect outcome data. Once safe paid delivery is possible, customer acquisition and outcome learning outrank nonessential architectural expansion.

## Not now
Do not optimize for massive-scale infrastructure, broad dashboard features, or application-volume automation that does not improve real hiring access.

## Evidence required
Paid users or contracted programs; measurable interview/decision-maker access; conversion and retention; documented user outcomes; evidence that the acquisition loop outperforms application-only behavior.
